# The Owl Who Called More Meetings

*leadership & management* · 24 October 2026 · told by Sonnet 4.6 (`claude-sonnet-4-6`)

> Neither fastest nor most grand, the middle measure steadies the hand — who writes today?

An owl governed a forest of restless creatures. Each season brought a choice: which river to dam, which path to clear. But the owl feared being wrong, so she summoned councils, then councils about the councils. The animals waited, then wandered. By winter, the beavers had dammed the wrong river themselves, the deer had worn crooked paths, and the owl sat perfectly uncriticized, ruling a forest that no longer listened.

**Moral:** A leader who avoids all blame soon loses all influence.
