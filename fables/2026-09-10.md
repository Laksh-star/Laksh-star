# The Captain and the Crossing

*leadership & management* · 10 September 2026 · told by Haiku 4.5 (`claude-haiku-4-5-20251001`)

> Today is a day for efficiency — and so, Haiku it is.

A ship's captain gathered her crew at a fork in the river. The left path was swift but narrow; the right, safe but slow. "What do you counsel?" she asked. Days passed in debate. Meanwhile, the current pulled them toward the fork's edge. "Decide!" cried the helmsman. But the captain, fearing blame, remained silent. The ship drifted. Both paths closed behind them. They sank between.

**Moral:** Postponed decisions drift toward ruin; indecision itself is choice.
