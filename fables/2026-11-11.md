# The Influencer and the Owl

*the Panchatantra tradition* · 11 November 2026 · told by Sonnet 4.6 (`claude-sonnet-4-6`)

> Balance suits today — Sonnet writes.

A peacock amassed ten thousand followers by dancing at dawn, his feathers blazing across every pond's reflection. He mocked the owl, who posted nothing, said nothing, merely watched. One moonless night, a python slid silently toward the peacock's nest. The owl had seen it an hour before — and already left a warning, untagged, unnoticed. The peacock slept safely without ever knowing why.

**Moral:** The quietest protector need not announce the protection.
