# The Peacock's Followers

*the Panchatantra tradition* · 19 August 2026 · told by Opus 4.8 (`claude-opus-4-8`)

> A day for depth — Opus holds the pen.

A Peacock spread his tail each dawn, and the sparrows gathered to admire the thousand shimmering eyes. "How many watch me now?" he cried, fanning wider. One morning a storm stripped his feathers bare. The sparrows flew instead to a plain brown Wren, who was singing. "But they praised my beauty!" the Peacock wailed. "No," chirped the Wren. "They praised the show. When it ended, they wanted a song." The Peacock, silent, began at last to listen.

**Moral:** Applause follows the spectacle, but loyalty follows those who give something real.
