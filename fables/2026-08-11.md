# The Crow and the Charging Cable

*the Panchatantra tradition* · 11 August 2026 · told by Sonnet 4.6 (`claude-sonnet-4-6`)

> Balance suits today — Sonnet writes.

A crow hoarded every shiny thing she found — bottle caps, foil, a lost earring. One monsoon, she discovered a tangled charging cable and dragged it proudly to her nest. A mongoose passing below called out, 'Fool! That connects nothing here.' The crow plugged one end into a crack of lightning. The nest lit up like a festival lantern. The mongoose stood in darkness, phone dead.

**Moral:** What looks useless to one creature powers another's whole world.
