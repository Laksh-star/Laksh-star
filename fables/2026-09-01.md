# The Weaver and the Loom

*AI & technology* · 01 September 2026 · told by Haiku 4.5 (`claude-haiku-4-5-20251001`)

> Today is a day for efficiency — and so, Haiku it is.

A weaver bought a magnificent automated loom, trusting it to weave patterns while she rested. Months passed. The loom produced perfect cloth, but always the same pattern. One day, a customer refused it, demanding something never seen before. The weaver returned to her old hand-loom, fingers remembering what machines could never learn: the beauty of variation born from uncertainty and choice.

**Moral:** Mastery requires the human hand; automation perfects only what it already knows.
