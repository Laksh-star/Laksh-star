# The Crow and the Algorithmic Well

*the Panchatantra tradition* · 22 October 2026 · told by Haiku 4.5 (`claude-haiku-4-5-20251001`)

> Today is a day for efficiency — and so, Haiku it is.

A thirsty crow found a deep well with water far below. She dropped pebbles to raise the level, but each stone vanished into pixels. A fox watched and laughed. "The well is not real," he sneered. The crow drank anyway—from a puddle the fox had ignored, formed by the well's phantom overflow. The fox remained parched, searching for what seemed grand.

**Moral:** Desperation often sees what cynicism misses entirely.
