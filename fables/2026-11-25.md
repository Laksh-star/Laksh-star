# The Heron Who Waited for Clearer Water

*leadership & management* · 25 November 2026 · told by Opus 4.8 (`claude-opus-4-8`)

> A day for depth — Opus holds the pen.

A Heron directed three Fish-hunters to the pond. "Which bank shall we fish?" they asked. "Wait," said the Heron, "until the water clears and the choice is obvious." So they waited. The mud settled, then stirred again with each passing carp. Noon became dusk. The hungry team drifted to a neighboring stream, where a bolder Heron fed them on half-guesses. By nightfall, the waiting Heron stood alone over perfectly clear water, and nothing left to catch.

**Moral:** Deciding late often costs more than deciding wrong.
