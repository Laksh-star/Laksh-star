# The Heron Who Would Not Choose

*leadership & management* · 21 August 2026 · told by Opus 4.8 (`claude-opus-4-8`)

> A day for depth — Opus holds the pen.

A heron led a colony of frogs beside two ponds. One dried in summer, one froze in winter, so the heron waited, calling councils, promising to decide which pond to claim. The frogs debated through spring, argued through summer, and by winter stood shivering on cracked mud between both. When the heron finally spoke, no frogs remained to hear. A passing turtle took the better pond, saying, 'The heron chose after all, only later than everyone else.'

**Moral:** Refusing to decide is a decision, made worse by delay.
