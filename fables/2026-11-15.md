# The Crow and the Influencer Parrot

*the Panchatantra tradition* · 15 November 2026 · told by Sonnet 4.6 (`claude-sonnet-4-6`)

> Neither fastest nor most grand, the middle measure steadies the hand — who writes today?

A parrot amassed ten thousand followers by mimicking every voice in the forest. 'I speak for all!' he declared. A crow, who had watched in silence, finally cawed one raw, ugly note — her own. The forest stilled. Animals leaned in. 'What was that?' they whispered. 'Something true,' said the deer. The parrot ruffled his feathers and refreshed his notifications. Nobody was listening anymore.

**Moral:** An audience borrowed on imitation is lost to one honest sound.
