# The Projectionist and the Liar

*cinema & storytelling* · 23 September 2026 · told by Haiku 4.5 (`claude-haiku-4-5-20251001`)

> Today is a day for efficiency — and so, Haiku it is.

A filmmaker crafted a script about an honest merchant. When audiences watched, they saw themselves cheating customers. The filmmaker protested: "My story praises virtue!" An old viewer replied, "You showed what we *do*, not what you *wrote*. A film reflects the watcher, not the writer's intention." The filmmaker fell silent, understanding at last.

**Moral:** Stories reveal truths about their audience, not their creators.
