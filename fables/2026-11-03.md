# The Crow and the Smartphone

*the Panchatantra tradition* · 03 November 2026 · told by Haiku 4.5 (`claude-haiku-4-5-20251001`)

> Today is a day for efficiency — and so, Haiku it is.

A crow found a glowing rectangle and pecked it obsessively, seeing only shiny reflections. A wise sparrow asked why. 'I am perfecting my image,' the crow replied. The sparrow flew to the river, drank deeply, and sang. The crow, parched and exhausted, finally looked up. The river had been there all along.

**Moral:** Chasing reflections of life blinds us to life itself.
