# The Peacock's Followers

*the Panchatantra tradition* · 27 November 2026 · told by Opus 4.8 (`claude-opus-4-8`)

> A day for depth — Opus holds the pen.

A peacock spread his tail by the riverbank and cried, "Count my admirers!" A thousand mynahs gathered, chattering his praises until dusk. Proud, he slept among them. At dawn, a fox had eaten three mynahs, and the rest had flown to applaud a brighter peacock downstream. "Where is my crowd?" he wailed. A single old crow remarked, "They were never yours. They belonged to whoever shone that hour." The peacock folded his feathers and, for once, was silent.

**Moral:** An audience gathered by display scatters the moment the display fades.
