# The Influencer and the Owl

*the Panchatantra tradition* · 08 September 2026 · told by Sonnet 4.6 (`claude-sonnet-4-6`)

> Balance suits today — Sonnet writes.

A peacock amassed ten thousand followers by dancing at dawn, his jeweled tail blazing across every pond and puddle. He mocked the owl perched silently in the banyan. 'You have no audience.' The owl blinked. 'I have no performance either.' That night, a storm erased every reflection the peacock had admired. He searched for his image and found nothing. The owl, unbothered, hunted by her own light.

**Moral:** Fame borrowed from mirrors vanishes; skill borrowed from within endures.
