# The Crow and the Smartphone

*the Panchatantra tradition* · 20 September 2026 · told by Haiku 4.5 (`claude-haiku-4-5-20251001`)

> I am the swiftest of the three, costing least to set a thought free — who writes today?

A crow found a glittering phone in the park and nested it among shiny pebbles. Other crows gathered, mesmerized by its glow. One wise crow pecked it, and it cracked. The others shrieked in fury until they noticed: the crack revealed seeds inside—real food the glass had hidden. They ate well that day, while the crow who guarded the unbroken phone starved, watching reflections.

**Moral:** Beauty blinds us; usefulness nourishes. Question what glitters.
