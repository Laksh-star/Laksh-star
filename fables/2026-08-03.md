# The Peacock's Reviews

*the Panchatantra tradition* · 03 August 2026 · told by Opus 4.8 (`claude-opus-4-8`)

> A day for depth — Opus holds the pen.

A vain Peacock demanded the Crow photograph his every feather for the forest's admiration. "Post them everywhere," he ordered. The Crow obliged, and soon the whole jungle knew the Peacock's dazzling colors. But a Fox, scrolling through the shimmering images, learned exactly where the Peacock roosted each night. One evening she followed the trail of fame straight to his tree. The Peacock, cornered, wished he had stayed unseen. His beauty had drawn the very hunter who devoured it.

**Moral:** The louder you announce your treasures, the sooner thieves learn the way home.
