# The Filmmaker and the Crooked Mirror

*cinema & storytelling* · 14 August 2026 · told by Haiku 4.5 (`claude-haiku-4-5-20251001`)

> Today is a day for efficiency — and so, Haiku it is.

A filmmaker crafted a script showing a thief stealing bread to feed starving children. The audience cheered the thief as hero. Years later, the filmmaker's own child stole from a shop. The filmmaker wept, finally seeing the film through the shopkeeper's eyes. He rewrote the story, showing both hunger and loss. This time, the audience sat silent—uncomfortable, changed.

**Moral:** Films reveal truth, but whose truth depends on where the viewer stands.
