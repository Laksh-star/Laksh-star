# The Captain and the Crossing

*leadership & management* · 18 September 2026 · told by Haiku 4.5 (`claude-haiku-4-5-20251001`)

> Today is a day for efficiency — and so, Haiku it is.

A ship's captain saw storm clouds gathering. His first mate urged turning north; the navigator insisted on waiting. The captain, unwilling to disappoint either, ordered the ship to drift. By dawn, the storm had passed—but the current had carried them onto hidden rocks. As the hull cracked, the captain realized the cost of his choice was far greater than the cost of making one.

**Moral:** Deciding poorly beats deciding never; indecision costs most of all.
