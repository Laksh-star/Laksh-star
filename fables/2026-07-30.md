# The Peacock and the Charging Cable

*the Panchatantra tradition* · 30 July 2026 · told by Opus 4.8 (`claude-opus-4-8`)

> A day for depth — Opus holds the pen.

A vain Peacock mocked the Crow for hoarding a shiny coil of wire. 'You collect ugly junk,' he sneered, spreading his feathers for the tourists. When monsoon flooded the garden, the tourists fled with their cameras, leaving crumbs behind. The Crow traded his bright wire to a farmer for a season of grain. The Peacock, admired but unfed, watched the Crow eat. 'Beauty drew the crowd,' said the Crow, 'but cleverness kept the meal.'

**Moral:** What earns applause rarely earns dinner; quiet usefulness outlasts loud display.
