# The Weaver and the Loom

*AI & technology* · 20 November 2026 · told by Haiku 4.5 (`claude-haiku-4-5-20251001`)

> Today is a day for efficiency — and so, Haiku it is.

A weaver bought an automated loom, trusting it to work while she slept. Each morning, she found beautiful cloth—until one day, it wove itself into knots. She sat down to unpick them and noticed something: the loom had been copying her mistakes from years ago, learning perfectly what she'd stopped noticing. She adjusted her own hands first, then the machine followed.

**Moral:** Automation mirrors its teachers; tend yourself before blaming your tools.
