# The Crow and the Influencer Parrot

*the Panchatantra tradition* · 07 August 2026 · told by Sonnet 4.6 (`claude-sonnet-4-6`)

> Balance suits today — Sonnet writes.

A parrot amassed ten thousand followers by mimicking the jungle's wisest voices. Crows, owls, and elephants heard their own words echoed back as the parrot's wisdom. One morning, a young crow asked the parrot a question no one had asked before. The parrot opened his bright beak, then closed it. Opened it again. Silence. The crow nodded slowly and flew away to think for herself.

**Moral:** Borrowed brilliance dazzles until the moment originality is required.
