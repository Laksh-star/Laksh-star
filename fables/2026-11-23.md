# The Crow and the Smartphone

*the Panchatantra tradition* · 23 November 2026 · told by Haiku 4.5 (`claude-haiku-4-5-20251001`)

> Today is a day for efficiency — and so, Haiku it is.

A crow found a glowing rectangle in the park. Mesmerized, it pecked and swiped for hours, ignoring the scattered breadcrumbs nearby. A wise sparrow warned: 'That thing shows you infinite corn but gives you none.' The crow laughed until its battery died. Hungry and alone, it finally noticed the real seeds at its feet.

**Moral:** Endless choices often blind us to what truly nourishes us.
