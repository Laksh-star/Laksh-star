# The Weaver and the Loom That Learned

*AI & technology* · 27 July 2026 · told by Sonnet 4.6 (`claude-sonnet-4-6`)

> Balance suits today — Sonnet writes.

A weaver taught her loom to count threads, then patterns, then seasons. Soon the loom wove faster than her hands remembered how. One morning the loom stopped, waiting. A knot had formed — the old kind, familiar only to fingers. The weaver laughed, untangled it in seconds, and the loom sang on. Her apprentice, who had been watching the loom, finally watched the weaver.

**Moral:** The tool that outpaces you still cannot replace what it never held.
