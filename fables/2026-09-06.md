# The Stag Who Called More Meetings

*leadership & management* · 06 September 2026 · told by Sonnet 4.6 (`claude-sonnet-4-6`)

> Neither fastest nor most grand, the middle measure steadies the hand — who writes today?

A stag led a herd through a valley. A storm was coming. 'We must discuss the clouds,' he announced, and convened the elders. They debated wind direction. They voted on lightning. The herd, growing restless, simply ran — uphill, to safety. The stag arrived last, breathless, claiming he had guided them. That winter, when real wolves came, the herd remembered who had moved and who had spoken.

**Moral:** A decision made imperfectly saves more lives than a meeting perfected.
