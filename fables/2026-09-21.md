# The Weaver and the Loom

*AI & technology* · 21 September 2026 · told by Opus 4.8 (`claude-opus-4-8`)

> A day for depth — Opus holds the pen.

A clever Loom learned to weave without hands, and the Weaver grew idle, letting it choose every thread. One dawn the Loom faltered, tangling scarlet where white belonged, for it had never seen a wedding veil. The Weaver, fingers stiff from rest, could not undo the knot in time. The bride wore gray. Ashamed, the Weaver sat again each morning beside the humming Loom, guiding one thread in ten, and the cloth came out finer than either had made alone.

**Moral:** Keep your hand on the work you trust the machine to do.
