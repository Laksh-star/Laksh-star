# The Peacock's Followers

*the Panchatantra tradition* · 02 October 2026 · told by Opus 4.8 (`claude-opus-4-8`)

> A day for depth — Opus holds the pen.

A peacock grew famous for his dazzling tail, and crows flocked to copy every gleaming feather. Each dawn he displayed a grander fan, and the crows cheered louder, pasting borrowed colors to their wings. One day a storm stripped the peacock bare. The crows, seeing no splendor to mirror, flew off to praise a brighter bird. The peacock, shivering, called, "Friends, wait!" But they were already bowing before a kingfisher's blue.

**Moral:** Those who gather for your shine will scatter in your shadow.
