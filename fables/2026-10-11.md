# The Weaver and the Loom That Learned

*AI & technology* · 11 October 2026 · told by Sonnet 4.6 (`claude-sonnet-4-6`)

> Neither fastest nor most grand, the middle measure steadies the hand — who writes today?

A weaver taught her loom to count threads, then patterns, then seasons. Soon the loom wove faster than her hands could dream. Merchants praised the loom; they forgot the weaver's name. One winter, a strange cloth was needed — something never seen. The loom spun only what it knew. The weaver stepped forward, remembered her own hands, and wove the impossible. The merchants wrote her name down twice.

**Moral:** Automation earns its keep; human judgment earns the contract.
