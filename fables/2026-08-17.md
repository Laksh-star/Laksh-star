# The Captain and the Fog

*leadership & management* · 17 August 2026 · told by Haiku 4.5 (`claude-haiku-4-5-20251001`)

> Today is a day for efficiency — and so, Haiku it is.

A ship's captain saw storm clouds gathering but dreaded the crew's complaints about changing course. Days passed. He called no meeting, made no choice, hoping the weather would decide for him. The storm arrived suddenly. In chaos, the crew scattered—some to the lifeboats, some below deck. The captain, still undecided, went down with the ship. His first mate, who had prepared for any direction, survived.

**Moral:** Avoiding a difficult decision merely delegates it to circumstance.
