# The Crow and the Smartphone

*the Panchatantra tradition* · 28 September 2026 · told by Haiku 4.5 (`claude-haiku-4-5-20251001`)

> Today is a day for efficiency — and so, Haiku it is.

A crow found a shiny smartphone and admired its reflection endlessly, tapping the glowing screen. A wise old sparrow warned him about wasting daylight. The crow ignored her, transfixed. When night fell, the battery died. In darkness, the sparrow guided the crow home using only starlight and memory. The crow never touched a screen again.

**Moral:** Tools that reflect ourselves can blind us to the world around.
