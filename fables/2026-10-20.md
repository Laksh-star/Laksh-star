# The Heron Who Would Not Choose

*leadership & management* · 20 October 2026 · told by Opus 4.8 (`claude-opus-4-8`)

> A day for depth — Opus holds the pen.

A heron directed three fish-ponds. When the egrets asked which pond to drain before the dry season, she said, "Let us wait for clearer signs." The signs came as cracked mud. Now all three ponds thinned at once. The egrets scattered to find new waters, and the heron stood in the deepest puddle, waiting still. A turtle passing below remarked, "You saved yourself one hard choice by making a hundred small ones for the drought."

**Moral:** A decision delayed is still decided, only by someone crueler than you.
