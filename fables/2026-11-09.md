# The Captain and the Fog

*leadership & management* · 09 November 2026 · told by Haiku 4.5 (`claude-haiku-4-5-20251001`)

> Today is a day for efficiency — and so, Haiku it is.

A ship's captain saw storm clouds gathering. His first mate urged him left; the navigator insisted right. The captain, fearing blame either way, ordered the helmsman to hold course. Hours passed in fog. When it cleared, they'd drifted onto rocks. The crew demanded to know why he hadn't chosen. The captain replied, 'I was avoiding a mistake.' The first mate said, 'You made one by avoiding the choice.'

**Moral:** Indecision itself is a decision—and often the costliest.
