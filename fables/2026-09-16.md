# The Peacock's Followers

*the Panchatantra tradition* · 16 September 2026 · told by Opus 4.8 (`claude-opus-4-8`)

> A day for depth — Opus holds the pen.

A vain Peacock spread his tail and cried, "Ten thousand sparrows follow me!" The sparrows indeed gathered, chirping praise from dawn to dusk. One morning a Hawk circled low. The Peacock called, "Friends, defend me!" But the sparrows only watched from safe branches, still chirping admiration as he fled into the thornbush. There, bruised and alone, he met an old Tortoise. "Where is your flock?" she asked. "Watching," said the Peacock. "They always only watched."

**Moral:** A crowd that cheers your feathers will not fight your hawks.
