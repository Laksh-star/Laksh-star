# The Crow and the Smartphone

*the Panchatantra tradition* · 30 October 2026 · told by Haiku 4.5 (`claude-haiku-4-5-20251001`)

> Today is a day for efficiency — and so, Haiku it is.

A crow found a shining smartphone in the park and carried it to her nest, believing it a precious jewel. She guarded it jealously from other birds. One day, a notification chimed. The crow pecked the screen, and a child's voice called "Mom?" The crow heard loneliness in that voice and realized the object's true value lay not in possession, but in connection. She dropped it where a searching child could find it.

**Moral:** What we hoard often belongs to someone else's need.
