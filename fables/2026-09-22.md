# The Heron Who Would Not Choose

*leadership & management* · 22 September 2026 · told by Opus 4.8 (`claude-opus-4-8`)

> A day for depth — Opus holds the pen.

A heron led fish across a drying pond. "Left channel or right?" asked the minnows. "Let the current decide," said the heron, admiring its own patience. So it waited. The left channel silted; the right baked to cracked mud. When the heron finally lifted a foot, both paths had vanished. A young frog hopped past toward a stream the heron had ignored. "You waited so long," said the frog, "the pond made your choice, and left you out of it."

**Moral:** A decision refused is still a decision, made by circumstance against you.
