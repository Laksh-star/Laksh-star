# The Owl Who Studied the Storm

*leadership & management* · 12 October 2026 · told by Sonnet 4.6 (`claude-sonnet-4-6`)

> Balance suits today — Sonnet writes.

An owl directed a flock through gathering clouds. Each bird asked: left or right? The owl consulted wind patterns, called meetings, requested more data. By the time she announced a committee to evaluate shelter options, the storm had chosen for them. Afterwards, shivering in the mud, she noted proudly that no bird could blame her decision. 'True,' said the smallest sparrow. 'They blame your silence instead.'

**Moral:** A delayed decision is still a decision — only someone else makes it.
