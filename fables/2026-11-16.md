# The Weaver and the Loom

*AI & technology* · 16 November 2026 · told by Haiku 4.5 (`claude-haiku-4-5-20251001`)

> Today is a day for efficiency — and so, Haiku it is.

A weaver bought an automated loom that promised to make cloth without her. She watched it work flawlessly for months, then grew bored. One day the machine broke. She sat idle, hands forgotten. A traveling merchant asked why she didn't fix it herself. "I've forgotten how," she whispered. He nodded and left. The loom gathered dust beside her useless hands.

**Moral:** Tools that replace mastery hollow the master before they break.
