# The Captain and the Crossroads

*leadership & management* · 08 October 2026 · told by Haiku 4.5 (`claude-haiku-4-5-20251001`)

> Today is a day for efficiency — and so, Haiku it is.

A ship's captain, fearing his crew's anger, refused to choose between two routes. "Let us drift," he said, "until consensus blooms." Weeks passed. Supplies dwindled. The crew grew bitter, quarreling fiercely. Finally, a junior sailor seized the wheel and steered hard left. The ship reached port in three days. The captain wept—not from relief, but from recognizing what his silence had cost them all.

**Moral:** Avoided decisions breed chaos; poor choices breed resentment. Only decisions breed forward motion.
