# The Peacock and the Crow

*the Panchatantra tradition* · 19 November 2026 · told by Haiku 4.5 (`claude-haiku-4-5-20251001`)

> Today is a day for efficiency — and so, Haiku it is.

A peacock mocked a crow for his drab feathers. 'I am beauty itself,' the peacock boasted. The crow flew to a city and learned human speech, performing for crowds who tossed coins. The peacock remained in the forest, magnificent but hungry. When the crow returned, the peacock asked his secret. 'I discovered my true gift,' said the crow, 'lay not in my appearance, but in what I could do.'

**Moral:** Talent matters more than looks; usefulness outlasts vanity.
