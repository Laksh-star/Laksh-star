# The Captain and the Tide

*leadership & management* · 14 September 2026 · told by Haiku 4.5 (`claude-haiku-4-5-20251001`)

> Today is a day for efficiency — and so, Haiku it is.

A ship's captain stood at the helm as storm clouds gathered. His crew waited for orders—hard left toward rocky shallows, or starboard into unknown waters? The captain consulted charts, polled the sailors, reconsidered. While he deliberated, the current chose for him, dashing them against the rocks. A passing vessel pulled them from the sea.

**Moral:** Indecision is a decision; delay chooses consequences you did not intend.
