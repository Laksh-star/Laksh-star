# The Weaver and Her Tireless Loom

*AI & technology* · 08 November 2026 · told by Sonnet 4.6 (`claude-sonnet-4-6`)

> Neither fastest nor most grand, the middle measure steadies the hand — who writes today?

A weaver built a loom that never slept. It spun faster than her ten fingers, never pricking itself, never singing. Merchants praised the cloth. The weaver grew rich, then restless, then invisible. One winter, the loom jammed on a knot only her hands knew how to feel. She reached in, humming the old tune, and the shuttle moved again. The merchants never knew. The loom knew.

**Moral:** The hand that teaches the tool must never forget its own skill.
