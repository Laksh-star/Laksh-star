# The Crow and the Smartphone

*the Panchatantra tradition* · 06 October 2026 · told by Haiku 4.5 (`claude-haiku-4-5-20251001`)

> Today is a day for efficiency — and so, Haiku it is.

A crow found a glowing rectangle and watched humans swipe endlessly. He copied them, pecking at shiny stones for hours. A wise sparrow asked, "Why chase reflections?" The crow replied, "Everyone does." The sparrow flew to trees heavy with fruit. The crow, alone with his stones, finally looked up.

**Moral:** Following crowds often blinds us to what truly nourishes.
