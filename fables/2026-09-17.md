# The Weaver and the Loom

*AI & technology* · 17 September 2026 · told by Haiku 4.5 (`claude-haiku-4-5-20251001`)

> Today is a day for efficiency — and so, Haiku it is.

A weaver bought a magical loom that wove cloth by itself. She grew idle, watching patterns emerge. One day the loom asked, 'Why do you stand there?' She replied, 'You weave now.' The loom stopped. 'I am only clever when your hands guide me,' it said. She returned to the threads. The cloth became beautiful again.

**Moral:** Tools amplify human intention; they cannot replace human purpose.
